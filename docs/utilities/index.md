# Utilities

A collection of small interactive tools for quick engineering calculations. Select a tool below to get started.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-speedometer:{ .lg .middle } &nbsp;**Terminal velocity calculator**

    ---

    Compute the terminal velocity, Reynolds number and drag coefficient of a
    spherical particle under drag, buoyancy and gravity — with a choice of
    classical drag models.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open](terminal-velocity.md)

-   :material-chart-bell-curve:{ .lg .middle } &nbsp;**Minimum fluidization & bed expansion**

    ---

    Estimate the minimum fluidization velocity from hindered-drag correlations
    and visualize bed expansion versus superficial velocity.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open](fluidization.md)

-   :material-server:{ .lg .middle } &nbsp;**HPC job cost calculator**

    ---

    Convert a job's core count and duration in hours into its allocation cost
    in core-hours and core-years.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open](hpc-cost.md)

-   :material-circle-multiple:{ .lg .middle } &nbsp;**DEM Rayleigh time & contact overlap**

    ---

    Compute the Rayleigh critical time for a DEM time step, and the maximum
    Hertzian contact overlap for a given impact velocity.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open](dem-contact.md)

-   :material-scatter-plot:{ .lg .middle } &nbsp;**Stokes number calculator**

    ---

    Compute a particle's relaxation time and its Stokes number relative to a
    characteristic flow time scale to assess how closely it follows the flow.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open](stokes-number.md)

</div>
