# Research

Our research combines high-performance computing, advanced numerical methods, and experimental validation to solve challenging multiphase and multiphysics problems in chemical engineering and manufacturing.

---

## Research Areas

<div class="research-grid" markdown>

<div class="research-card" markdown>
<div class="research-card-header">
<div class="research-card-icon">
<img src="../assets/logo_research_topics/particles.svg" alt="CFD icon">
</div>
<p class="research-card-title">Computational Fluid Dynamics</p>
</div>
<p class="research-card-description">
High-order finite element methods for single-phase and multiphase flows. We specialize in implicit LES for turbulent flows and CFD-DEM coupling for particle-laden systems in applications ranging from fluidized beds to additive manufacturing.
</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card" markdown>
<div class="research-card-header">
<div class="research-card-icon">
<img src="../assets/logo_research_topics/particles.svg" alt="Multiphase icon">
</div>
<p class="research-card-title">Multiphase Flow Physics</p>
</div>
<p class="research-card-description">
Simulation and experiments to understand fluid-fluid and particle-laden flows. This knowledge guides the design and optimization of unit operations and manufacturing processes.
</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card" markdown>
<div class="research-card-header">
<div class="research-card-icon">
<img src="../assets/logo_research_topics/multiphysics.svg" alt="Multiphysics icon">
</div>
<p class="research-card-title">Multiphysics Simulations</p>
</div>
<p class="research-card-description">
Coupling thermal, electromagnetic, fluid, and reactive phenomena for applications including catalytic reactors, additive manufacturing, active cooling, and microwave-assisted processes.
</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card" markdown>
<div class="research-card-header">
<div class="research-card-icon">
<img src="../assets/logo_research_topics/numerical_methods.svg" alt="HPC icon">
</div>
<p class="research-card-title">High-Performance Computing</p>
</div>
<p class="research-card-description">
Scalable numerical strategies for large-scale multiphase and granular flow simulations, optimized for modern parallel architectures.
</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card" markdown>
<div class="research-card-header">
<div class="research-card-icon">
<img src="../assets/logo_research_topics/numerical_methods.svg" alt="Numerical methods icon">
</div>
<p class="research-card-title">Numerical Methods</p>
</div>
<p class="research-card-description">
Advanced schemes including high-order CFD-DEM, discontinuous Galerkin methods, and accurate interface treatments for multiphase flows. Focus on efficient discretization and stabilization strategies.
</p>
</div>

</div>

## Active Projects

<div class="project-grid" markdown>

<div class="project-card" markdown>
<p class="project-card-title">Multiphase Simulation for Powder Bed Additive Manufacturing</p>
<p class="project-card-description">
Developing a comprehensive framework integrating DEM for powder spreading and VOF-based thermo-fluid modeling for melt pool dynamics in Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF). The goal is to predict powder bed uniformity and melt pool geometry under various laser parameters.
</p>
<p class="project-card-team"><strong>Team:</strong> A. Alphonius, H. Papillon Laroche, O. Gaboriault</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card" markdown>
<p class="project-card-title">Graphite Spheroidization</p>
<p class="project-card-description">
Improving morphological control and energy efficiency in producing battery-grade anode materials. Using CFD-DEM to simulate particle interactions during shaping and optimize process parameters for lithium-ion cell manufacturing.
</p>
<p class="project-card-team"><strong>Team:</strong> C. Wilson, J. Tremblay</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card" markdown>
<p class="project-card-title">Process Intensification of Multiphysics Systems</p>
<p class="project-card-description">
Developing numerical tools for energy-efficient chemical reactors through three directions: electromagnetic-thermal modeling for microwave processes, neural network acceleration for reactive porous media, and active cooling via impinging jets.
</p>
<p class="project-card-team"><strong>Team:</strong> O. Marquis, V. Oliveira Ferreira, O. Guévremont, W. Mainville</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card" markdown>
<p class="project-card-title">Ultrasonic Particle Control</p>
<p class="project-card-description">
Developing an ultrasonic-based system for microplastic detection and characterization in aquatic environments. Using acoustic radiation forces to concentrate particles for Raman spectroscopy analysis through resolved CFD-DEM simulations.
</p>
<p class="project-card-team"><strong>Team:</strong> J. Lamouche</p>
</div>

</div>

## Past Projects

<div class="project-grid" markdown>

<div class="project-card" markdown>
<p class="project-card-title">Radioactive Particle Tracking (RPT)</p>
<p class="project-card-description">
Developed a model-free RPT technique using neural networks for characterizing hydrodynamics in multiphase reactors. Validated in stirred tank flows with strong agreement to CFD simulations.
</p>
<p class="project-card-team"><strong>Duration:</strong> 2019–2024</p>
